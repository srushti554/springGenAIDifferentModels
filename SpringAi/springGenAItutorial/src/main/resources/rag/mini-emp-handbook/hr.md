# Employee HR Handbook

## Leave, Vacation & HR Support Policies

**Company:** Acme Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Document Owner:** Human Resources Department

---

## 1. Purpose

This handbook explains the company's leave and vacation policies and provides employees with information about how to request leave and contact the HR team.

Employees should review this handbook before submitting a leave request. Leave approval is subject to company policy, applicable laws, business requirements, and manager approval.

---

# 2. Leave Policies

## 2.1 Annual / Vacation Leave

Employees receive **18 days of paid vacation leave per calendar year**.

### Policy

* Vacation leave can be used for personal travel, rest, family commitments, or other personal reasons.
* Employees should submit vacation requests at least **5 working days in advance**.
* Vacation requests are subject to manager approval.
* Employees should check their available leave balance before submitting a request.
* Unused vacation leave may be carried forward according to the company's applicable carry-forward rules.
* Employees should coordinate planned vacations with their team to avoid disruption to critical business activities.

### Example

An employee who wants to take vacation from Monday through Wednesday should submit a leave request covering all three working days.

---

## 2.2 Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to **12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year**.

### Policy

* Sick leave may be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness or injury.
* Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible.
* For extended illness, HR may request supporting medical documentation.
* Sick leave should not be used for vacation or personal activities.
* Unused sick leave does not have a cash value unless specifically required by applicable law or company policy.

### Medical Documentation

For sick leave of **3 or more consecutive working days**, the employee may be required to provide appropriate medical documentation.

---

## 2.3 Casual / Personal Leave

Employees receive **6 days of casual leave per calendar year**.

### Policy

Casual leave may be used for:

* Personal appointments
* Family commitments
* Urgent personal matters
* Short-term personal requirements

Employees should normally request casual leave at least **1 working day in advance**.

For emergencies, employees should notify their manager as soon as possible.

---

## 2.4 Bereavement Leave

Employees may take **5 paid working days of bereavement leave** following the death of an immediate family member.

Immediate family members generally include:

* Spouse
* Parents
* Children
* Siblings

Employees should contact HR if they require additional time away from work.

---

## 2.5 Parental Leave

### Maternity Leave

Eligible employees may receive maternity leave in accordance with applicable law and company policy.

Employees should contact HR as early as possible to discuss:

* Expected leave dates
* Required documentation
* Benefits during leave
* Return-to-work arrangements

### Paternity / Partner Leave

Eligible employees may receive **10 paid working days** of paternity or partner leave following the birth or adoption of a child.

Employees should coordinate the expected leave dates with their manager and HR.

---

## 2.6 Adoption Leave

Employees who legally adopt a child may be eligible for parental/adoption leave in accordance with applicable law and company policy.

Employees should contact HR to confirm eligibility and required documentation.

---

## 2.7 Unpaid Leave

Employees may request unpaid leave when they have exhausted applicable paid leave or when the reason for absence is not covered by another leave category.

### Policy

* Unpaid leave requires manager and HR approval.
* Employees should submit the request before the leave begins whenever possible.
* Approval depends on the circumstances and business requirements.
* Employees should discuss extended unpaid leave with HR before making travel or other commitments.

---

# 3. Leave Request Process

Employees should follow these steps when requesting leave:

1. Check the available leave balance.
2. Discuss planned leave with the manager when appropriate.
3. Submit the leave request through the company's HR/leave management system.
4. Wait for manager approval.
5. Inform relevant team members about planned absence when necessary.
6. Set an appropriate out-of-office message for extended leave.

For emergency leave, employees should notify their manager as soon as possible and submit the leave request afterward.

---

# 4. Public Holidays

The company publishes an annual holiday calendar at the beginning of each calendar year.

The holiday calendar may vary based on:

* Location
* Applicable local regulations
* Business requirements
* Company operations

Employees should refer to the official HR holiday calendar for the current year's holidays.

---

# 5. Leave Carry-Forward

Vacation leave may be carried forward to the following calendar year subject to company limits.

**Maximum carry-forward:** 10 days.

Sick leave and other leave categories may have different carry-forward or expiry rules.

Employees should contact HR if they are unsure about their leave balance or carry-forward eligibility.

---

# 6. Leave During Notice Period

Employees serving a notice period should discuss planned leave with their manager and HR.

Leave during the notice period may be subject to additional approval requirements depending on the type of leave and applicable company policy.

Statutory leave rights remain subject to applicable law.

---

# 7. Unauthorized Absence

Employees are expected to inform their manager or HR when they are unable to report to work.

Repeated unauthorized absence or failure to follow the leave-request process may result in disciplinary action in accordance with company policy.
