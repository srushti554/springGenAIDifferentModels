# Office & Workplace Handbook

**Company:** Acme Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Document Owner:** Administration & Facilities Department

---

## 1. Purpose

This handbook provides employees with basic information about office facilities, workplace rules, attendance, safety, and facilities support.

---

## 2. Office Working Hours

**Standard Office Hours:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM

Employees are expected to follow their team's assigned working schedule.

Employees working remotely or under flexible arrangements should follow the schedule agreed with their manager.

---

## 3. Office Access

Employees must use their assigned employee ID card or access badge to enter the office.

### Guidelines

* Do not share your access card with another person.
* Report lost or damaged access cards to Administration immediately.
* Visitors must register at the reception desk.
* Employees should not allow unauthorized individuals to enter restricted areas.

---

## 4. Workspaces

Employees are expected to maintain a clean and professional workspace.

Please:

* Keep desks reasonably clean.
* Avoid blocking walkways or emergency exits.
* Take care of company furniture and equipment.
* Use meeting rooms responsibly.
* Leave shared spaces clean after use.

---

## 5. Meeting Rooms

Meeting rooms should be reserved through the company's room-booking system.

Employees should:

* Cancel unused reservations.
* Avoid exceeding the booked time.
* Leave the room clean.
* Report equipment problems to Facilities.

---

## 6. Office Facilities

The office may provide facilities such as:

* Pantry and drinking water
* Cafeteria
* Meeting rooms
* Parking
* Rest areas
* Printing facilities
* Employee transportation, where applicable

Employees should follow the specific rules displayed for each facility.

---

## 7. Health & Safety

Employees must follow workplace safety instructions.

In an emergency:

1. Remain calm.
2. Follow instructions from the Facilities/Security team.
3. Use designated emergency exits.
4. Do not use elevators during a fire emergency.
5. Assemble at the designated emergency assembly point.

Report unsafe conditions to Facilities immediately.

---

## 8. Workplace Conduct

Employees are expected to maintain a respectful and professional workplace.

Employees should not engage in:

* Harassment
* Discrimination
* Threatening behavior
* Violence
* Intentionally damaging company property

Workplace concerns can be reported to HR or the appropriate company representative.

---

## 9. Food & Pantry

Employees should maintain cleanliness in shared food and pantry areas.

Please:

* Clean up after yourself.
* Dispose of food waste properly.
* Do not leave perishable food unattended for extended periods.
* Follow cafeteria and pantry rules.

---

## 10. Parking

Employees using company parking must follow the designated parking rules.

Parking access may require registration and an employee ID or vehicle pass.

Employees should contact Facilities for parking-related issues.
