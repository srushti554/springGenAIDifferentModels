# Business Travel Handbook

**Company:** Acme Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Document Owner:** Travel & Administration Department

---

## 1. Purpose

This handbook explains the company's business travel policies, booking procedures, expenses, and travel support available to employees.

---

## 2. Business Travel Approval

Employees must obtain manager approval before booking business travel.

Travel requests should include:

* Travel destination
* Purpose of travel
* Travel dates
* Estimated expenses
* Customer or office location, if applicable

Employees should not make non-refundable bookings before receiving the required approval.

---

## 3. Travel Booking

Business travel should be booked through the company's approved travel portal or travel agency.

The company may cover:

* Flights or trains
* Hotel accommodation
* Local transportation
* Business travel-related expenses

Employees should select reasonable and cost-effective travel options within company policy.

---

## 4. Flights & Train Travel

Employees should generally book the lowest reasonable fare that meets business requirements.

### Guidelines

* Economy class should be used for standard business travel unless otherwise approved.
* Business-class travel requires appropriate approval.
* Employees should book travel as early as reasonably possible.
* Changes or cancellations should be avoided where possible.

---

## 5. Hotel Accommodation

Employees should use company-approved hotels or the corporate travel portal when available.

Hotel expenses should remain within the applicable company travel limits.

Personal expenses such as minibar purchases, entertainment, and room upgrades may not be reimbursed unless specifically approved.

---

## 6. Local Transportation

Reasonable transportation expenses incurred for business purposes may be reimbursed.

Examples include:

* Airport transfers
* Taxis
* Ride-sharing services
* Public transportation
* Rental vehicles, where approved

Employees should retain receipts where required.

---

## 7. Travel Expenses

Employees may claim eligible business travel expenses according to the company's reimbursement policy.

Typical reimbursable expenses include:

* Transportation
* Accommodation
* Business meals
* Approved travel-related expenses

Employees must submit expense claims within **15 days after completing the trip**.

---

## 8. Travel Safety

Employees should follow reasonable safety precautions while traveling for business.

Employees should:

* Keep company equipment secure.
* Avoid sharing confidential company information in public places.
* Follow local laws and regulations.
* Keep emergency contact information available.
* Report travel-related emergencies to the company as soon as possible.

---

## 9. International Travel

Employees traveling internationally may need additional approval and documentation.

Before international travel, employees should confirm:

* Passport validity
* Visa requirements
* Travel insurance
* Required business approvals
* Local entry requirements

Employees should contact HR or the Travel Desk if assistance is required.

---

## 10. Travel Cancellation

If a business trip is cancelled or changed, employees should notify their manager and Travel Desk as soon as possible.

Employees should use refundable or changeable bookings where required by company policy.

## 12. Quick Reference

**Need to travel for work:** Get manager approval first.

**Need to book a flight/hotel:** Use the approved travel portal or Travel Desk.

**Need reimbursement:** Submit an expense claim with required receipts.

**Trip cancelled:** Notify your manager and Travel Desk immediately.

**Travel emergency:** Contact the Travel Hotline and Security when appropriate.

**International travel:** Confirm visa, passport, insurance, and required approvals before booking.

---

## 13. Important Notice

Business travel expenses are reimbursed only when they comply with applicable company policies and approval requirements.

Where this handbook conflicts with the official company travel or expense policy, the official policy takes precedence.
