# Security Department Handbook

**Company:** Acme Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Document Owner:** Security Department

---

## 1. Purpose

This handbook explains basic workplace security policies, access rules, incident reporting procedures, and security contacts for employees.

---

## 2. Building Access

Employees must use their assigned access card or badge when entering the office.

### Guidelines

* Never share your access card with another person.
* Do not allow unauthorized people to enter through secured doors.
* Report lost or stolen access cards immediately.
* Visitors must register at reception and follow visitor procedures.
* Restricted areas may only be accessed by authorized employees.

---

## 3. Visitor Policy

All visitors must:

1. Register at the reception/security desk.
2. Provide required identification.
3. Wear a visitor badge while inside the office.
4. Remain with their host when required.
5. Return the visitor badge before leaving.

Employees are responsible for informing Security about expected visitors when required.

---

## 4. Workplace Security

Employees should remain alert and report suspicious activity.

Report situations such as:

* Unknown individuals in restricted areas
* Unattended suspicious packages
* Lost company property
* Unauthorized access attempts
* Threatening behavior
* Damage to security equipment

Do not attempt to physically confront a suspected security threat. Contact Security instead.

---

## 5. Lost & Found

Items found inside the office should be handed over to the Security or Reception desk.

Employees who lose personal or company property should report it to Security.

**Lost & Found Desk:** Office Security Desk

---

## 6. Emergency Procedures

In case of an emergency:

1. Stay calm.
2. Follow instructions from Security and emergency personnel.
3. Use designated emergency exits.
4. Do not use elevators during a fire emergency.
5. Proceed to the designated assembly point.
6. Do not re-enter the building until authorized.

---

## 7. Security Incident Reporting

Employees should immediately report security incidents to the Security team.

Examples include:

* Unauthorized entry
* Theft
* Lost access card
* Suspicious person
* Suspicious package
* Workplace violence
* Property damage
* Emergency situations

When reporting an incident, provide the location, approximate time, and a brief description of what happened.

---

## 8. Access Card Policy

Employees are responsible for their assigned access cards.

If a card is lost or stolen:

1. Notify Security immediately.
2. Security will deactivate the card when necessary.
3. Request a replacement card.

Employees must not lend their access card to colleagues or visitors.

## 10. Quick Reference

**Lost access card:** Contact Security immediately.

**Suspicious person:** Do not confront them; contact Security.

**Lost company property:** Report it to Security.

**Visitor issue:** Contact Reception or Security.

**Emergency:** Follow evacuation instructions and contact Security/emergency services.

**Security incident:** Report it immediately to the Security Desk.

## 11. Important Notice

Employees must follow all company security, access-control, emergency, and workplace safety policies.

Security procedures may vary depending on the office location and type of incident. When in doubt, contact the Security team for guidance.
